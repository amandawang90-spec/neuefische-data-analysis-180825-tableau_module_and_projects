# What we'll do in today's session:

- Understand key **business concepts** like KPIs and Business Intelligence (BI), and how they relate to data storytelling.
- Work in teams to define **KPI examples** and design **dashboard sketches** (using the avocado case) based on real-world scenarios.
- Discuss the role of a data analyst and how data supports better decision-making across an organization.
- Learn about the **Tableau ecosystem**—an overview of all major Tableau products and their purpose in the data journey.
- Explore **Tableau Public** as a platform for sharing and exploring data visualizations (exercise continues in the next session).
- Reflect on how Tableau fits into the broader landscape of BI tools, while focusing on storytelling and insights rather than just tool usage.

- Please check the presentations after the lecture for self study.
