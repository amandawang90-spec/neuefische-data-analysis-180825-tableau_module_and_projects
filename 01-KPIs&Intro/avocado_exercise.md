## Exercise
As a group, check the data described below and think about how you would like to orient your data analysis for this dataset. You could focus on the presented data only but you are absolutely free to think about any other data that they would like to add to it. You have 10-15 mins to discuss and you will be presenting your work afterwards.


__Avocados in stores in the US (top 10 resellers):__

**Sales:**  
Date - The date of the observation  
Average Price - the average price of a single avocado  
Type - conventional or organic  
Store Id - identifier of the store  
Store Location - location of the store (address, city, state)  
Total Volume - Total number of avocados sold  


**Buys:**  
Date - The date of the observation  
Average Price - the average price of a single avocado  
Type - conventional or organic  
Total Volume - total number of avocados bought  
Region - the location where the avocados were grown  
Producer Id - identifier of the producer  


### Note: 
there is no solution to this exercise - you might want to go in a very business oriented direction (optimization of the sales, predictions of the sales, identifying the right markets, etc.), avocado sales as proxy for different metrics (like inflation, or big mac price index) or sth more political (avocados production and how it affects the producers - mafia/crime activity influence).
