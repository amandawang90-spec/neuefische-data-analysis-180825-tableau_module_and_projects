## Tableau Storytelling & Advanced Visuals

- Learn how to create a **Tableau Story** by combining all your dashboards into a single narrative to present your insights effectively.  
- Adjust your story layout and design for a professional, readable presentation.  
- Practice **publishing your work** — learn how to save and share via Tableau Public or Server, and understand the role of **data extracts** when publishing.  
- Build a **Funnel Chart** to visualize process stages (like Orders → Shipped → Returned) and understand how sorting, color, and formatting can enhance clarity.   
- (Exercises coming up) You’ll extend your story by adding new dashboards and visual types to strengthen your analytical storytelling and presentation skills.
