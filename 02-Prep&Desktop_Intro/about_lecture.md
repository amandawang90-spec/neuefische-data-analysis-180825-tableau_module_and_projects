## Superstore Dataset
A sample retail dataset containing orders, customers, products, and regional data. It is commonly used to explore sales and profit patterns in Tableau.

## Connecting to Data
- **Live connection:** Direct link to the data source that updates automatically.
- **Extract:** A saved snapshot of the data that loads faster and works offline.

## Cleaning and Shaping Data
- **Rename fields:** Make field names more descriptive.
- **Hide fields:** Remove unused or irrelevant fields from view.
- **Pivot data:** Reshape columns into rows for easier analysis (e.g., convert multiple year columns into a single "Year" field).

## Basic Visualizations
- **Bar chart:** Compare values across categories.
- **Dual axis:** Display two related measures on one chart.
- **Color encoding:** Use color to distinguish categories or emphasize differences in values.

## Tableau Structure
- **Worksheet:** A single visualization or chart.
- **Dashboard:** A collection of multiple worksheets displayed together.
- **Story:** A sequence of dashboards or worksheets used to present insights.

## Saving Workbooks
- **.twb (Tableau Workbook):** Saves only the workbook instructions, not the data.
- **.twbx (Tableau Packaged Workbook):** Includes the workbook and its data, making it easier to share.
